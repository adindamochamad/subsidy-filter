NEAR’s reported active accounts are a count of distinct signer_account_id values. This note splits that count into signers who did something other than transact with a hot.tg receiver, and flags days where headcount spikes while transactions-per-signer collapse. Thresholds live in classify/config.py. The argument, including what cuts against it, is in THESIS.md.

How to read this

Net Demand does not subtract burst windows. It only subtracts signers whose monthly activity is exclusively with receivers matching %hot.tg. February and October therefore remain large on the Net Demand line. That is a finding, not a bug: those bursts are not HOT-only, and they are not attributed. The last month in the extract (2026-11) has a named receiver on 10 Nov; one attributed burst does not attribute the others. See THESIS.md.
